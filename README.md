## About

**The AI Guardian Cards** are a set of speculative design probes built from workshops in which teenagers imagined alternative futures for generative AI.

Through creative making, participants created **Guardians**: characters able to protect the values they considered important in a world increasingly shaped by AI. The cards bring these perspectives together into five value-driven Guardians.

The cards were developed as part of **[Tadaa](https://www.irenezanardi.com/tadaa/)**.

---

## Who is it for?

The cards are designed for:

- 🔬 Researchers
- 🎓 Educators
- 🎨 Designers
- 💬 Anyone interested in discussing the future of AI with young people

They can be used:

- In workshops
- In classrooms
- In design activities
- In group discussions
- In research studies

---

## What's in the Card Set?

The collection includes **five Guardian Cards**, each representing a value identified by teenagers during the workshops:

| Guardian | Value |
|-----------|--------|
| ✨ Guardian I | Positivity |
| 🧭 Guardian II | Clarity & Self-Reliance |
| 🎭 Guardian III | Expression |
| 🌍 Guardian IV | Openness & Diversity |
| ⚖️ Guardian V | Realness & Balance |

## How to Use the Cards

1. **Explore the five Guardians** and the values they represent.
2. **Use the reflection questions** to discuss how current AI systems support or challenge each value.
3. **Imagine alternatives** by considering what future technologies might look like if they actively protected these values.

The cards can be used individually or as a group activity and require no preparation.
